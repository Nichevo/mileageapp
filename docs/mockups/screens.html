App icon

Direction A is the chosen icon; B and C are kept for the record. Each is drawn as an Android adaptive icon (the artwork stays inside the inner 66% so round, squircle and square masks all work). The same mark is shown at launcher size and at notification size, where it has to survive as a single colour.

A · Gauge, nozzle to bolt (chosen)

A dial whose needle sweeps from a petrol pump to a lightning bolt: fuel economy today, EV later, and a trip computer's shape throughout. Reads at 24 px as a simple gauge.

How the screens connect

  1. First launch: sign in to Google, pick the Mileage sheet, preview the 10 rows, import. The dashboard then shows the real lifetime numbers.
  2. At the pump: Dashboard → Log a fill-up → Save. The row is queued and appended to the sheet when there is signal.
  3. Road trip: Trips tab → Start trip (type the odometer) → drive → End trip (type the odometer). Fill-ups saved during a trip become stops.
  4. Later: History shows everything, Export re-sends the sheet tabs or a Dropbox backup, and the EV appears as a second vehicle with the same screens in blue.
